# Presentation outline

1. **Problem:** deadlines compete for limited study time; an opaque schedule can create more stress.
2. **Project question:** turn subjects, topics, deadlines, and available hours into an adjustable plan.
3. **Audience and assumptions:** one student; estimates and due dates are provided by the learner; daily availability is explicit.
4. **Prototype walkthrough:** create account, enter topics, review overview, adjust daily hours, inspect plan, edit or complete tasks.
5. **How the planner works:** earliest deadline → student priority → difficulty; 45-minute sessions; 10-minute resets; short revision; no scheduling past deadlines.
6. **Responsible AI:** deterministic schedule first; no AI model or hidden planning logic. AI may later explain strategies, not make undisclosed decisions.
7. **Testing:** same-day and overlapping deadlines, insufficient availability, missing estimate defaults, completed tasks, and past-due work.
8. **Limitations and next steps:** no per-day calendar constraints yet; add calendar export and privacy/deployment hardening.
