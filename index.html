YOUR GUIDE TO A KINDER PLAN

How Studyflow works

1. Put it all down. Add each topic, your best estimate, a due date, and what matters most to you.

2. Get a transparent first draft. The planner uses clear, repeatable rules: earlier deadlines come first, then your priority and difficulty. No hidden AI scheduling.

3. Make it yours. Adjust available hours, change priorities, or mark work complete. The plan is a helpful suggestion — not a promise. It includes short breaks and flags work that may not fit.

4. Leave room for real life. Rest, accessibility needs, and unexpected events matter more than a schedule. Revisit the plan whenever you need to.
CLEAR EXPECTATIONS, NO SURPRISES

Privacy at Studyflow

Studyflow stores your account and study topics in a local SQLite file on the computer running this prototype. Your password is saved as a salted hash; it is not stored as plain text.

This educational prototype does not send your topics to an AI service. Google Fonts may be requested by your browser to display the selected typefaces; the system falls back to local fonts if they are unavailable.

Use a unique password and keep the project folder private. This prototype does not include account recovery or production-grade deployment safeguards.

Studyflow is a learning and planning prototype. Its schedule is an estimate and a suggestion, not a guarantee, a grade, or professional advice.

Review and adjust the plan to fit your actual commitments, wellbeing, and accessibility needs. You are responsible for keeping your estimates and deadlines current.

Do not expose the local development server to a public network or use this prototype to store sensitive information.
